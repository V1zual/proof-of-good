# Landing page plan (skill: landing-page-design)

Assumptions (all defaults accepted on 27 Oct): project Proof of Good for the PayPal AI Hackathon; one action "Try the full
flow in 2 minutes"; audience PayPal judges and donors abroad who want to help Ukraine but distrust strangers' fundraisers;
traffic from the Devpost page and the demo video; no testimonials (none exist, none invented), real demo numbers and
real research instead; dark minimal editorial; calm professional tone; English; desktop and mobile.

## 1. Page outline
1. Skip link + floating nav (How it works, Proof, FAQ, Campaigns + small "Try it" button)
2. Hero: headline, subheadline, one CTA, proof line, product replay panel (a real run, replayed)
3. Problem to solution (Give.org Donor Trust Report 2026 numbers)
4. Tagline reveal (B11)
5. Benefits (5)
6. How it works (3 steps)
7. Proof: live numbers from the public ledger + facts measured on the demo
8. FAQ (10) + FAQ schema
9. Risk reversal + final CTA (same as the hero)
10. Footer: campaigns, ledger, volunteer portal, source, privacy, terms

## 2. Hero copy
- **Headline:** Give to Ukrainian volunteers / and see every payment they make
- **Subheadline:** Your donation waits on PayPal and reaches the volunteer one budget line at a time, on a card whose every payment is checked by AI. If the money is misused, the rest comes back to you.
- **CTA:** Try the full flow in 2 minutes
- **Proof line:** Every card payment checked in about 8 seconds · Built on PayPal Orders, Payouts and Refunds

## 3. Benefits
- **No blank checks.** Only one budget line sits on the card at a time, so the most that can ever go wrong is one line, not the whole fund.
- **Every payment in public.** The bank reports each card payment and AI matches it to the purpose in about 8 seconds.
- **Proof unlocks the next part.** A photo of the item where it is used releases the next budget line. Nothing moves on promises.
- **Misuse stops at once.** Cash withdrawals, card to card transfers and gambling freeze the card, and PayPal refunds every donor's held share.
- **Volunteers start with zero savings.** The first line reaches the card as soon as it is funded.

## 4. How it works
1. **Give.** Pay with PayPal or any bank card. The money is held; nobody has it yet.
2. **Spend with the transparent card.** One budget line goes to the volunteer's campaign card. Every payment is checked as it happens.
3. **Prove and unlock.** A photo of what was bought accounts for the line and releases the next one. Misuse sends the rest back to donors.

## 5. FAQ
1. What if the volunteer spends the money on something else?
2. The volunteer has no money of their own. How do they buy anything?
3. What if the shop wants cash on delivery?
4. Can a volunteer fake the photo?
5. Do I need a PayPal account to give?
6. Which banks does the transparent card work with?
7. Is this real money?
8. Who checks the AI?
9. What happens if a campaign raises more than it needs?
10. Why Ukraine first?

## 6. SEO / AEO
**noindex** — a hackathon demo running on test money is time bound. Title and meta are still set for sharing:
title "Proof of Good · Give to volunteers and see every payment", description "Donations wait on PayPal and reach
volunteers one budget line at a time, on a card whose every payment is checked by AI. Misuse sends the rest back."
FAQ in plain question and answer form with FAQPage schema.

## 7. Layout
**B. Long form story** with a classic hero. The money flow is new and the audience is skeptical by default ("a stranger
will take my money"): the page has to explain the mechanism, prove it with real numbers and answer objections.
