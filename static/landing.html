The problem

People want to give. They just cannot see where the money goes.

After every disaster fake fundraisers appear, and honest volunteers lose donors with them. A bank jar shows how much came in. It never shows what was bought.

Proof of Good shows both. The volunteer spends from a transparent card, the bank reports each payment, and AI checks it before the next part of the money moves.

Why it works

Safe for donors. Fair to volunteers.

No blank checks

Only one budget line sits on the card at a time, so the most that can ever go wrong is one line, not the whole fund.

Every payment in public

The bank reports each card payment and AI matches it to the purpose in about 10 seconds.

Proof unlocks the next part

A photo of the item where it is used releases the next budget line. Nothing moves on promises.

Misuse stops at once

Cash withdrawals, card to card transfers and gambling freeze the card, and PayPal refunds every donor's held share.

Volunteers start with zero savings

The first line reaches the card as soon as it is funded. The proof comes after the purchase.

How it works

Three steps between a donation and a generator in a hospital.

01

Give

Pay with PayPal or any bank card. The money is held on PayPal and nobody has it yet.

02

Spend with the transparent card

One budget line goes to the volunteer's campaign card. The bank reports every payment and AI checks it as it happens.

03

Prove and unlock

A photo of what was bought accounts for the line and releases the next one. Misuse sends the rest back to donors.

Follow $12 from a donor to a bottle of water

  1. $12 donatedPaid with PayPal and held there. Nobody has it yet.
  2. $10 sent to the cardThe first budget line, water for the shelter, goes to the volunteer.
  3. −₴440 at a supermarketThe bank reports it and AI matches it to water in about 10 seconds.
  4. Photo of the waterTaken on site. It proves the line and unlocks the next one.
  5. $2 still heldIt waits for the next line, or goes back to the donor.

If the card pays for anything else, like a cash withdrawal, a transfer or gambling, it freezes on the spot and the $2 still held goes back to the donor through PayPal Refunds.

Proof

Numbers from the live demo, not promises.

Counted from the public ledger right now. The demo runs on the PayPal sandbox, so every number below is real API traffic with test money.

  • 12 merchant categories freeze the card on sight: cash, card to card transfers, crypto, gambling, bars and jewellery.
  • Every photo passes 5 to 7 checks: reuse, camera date, editing software, AI credentials, the item itself, a real photo, a match with the purchase.
  • A fake shop screenshot was rejected in 15.3 seconds, with the reasons shown to the volunteer and to donors.
  • Every PayPal id in the ledger is a real sandbox transaction you can look up, and the code is open on GitHub.
