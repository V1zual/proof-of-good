Privacy

What this demo keeps about you

Proof of Good is a hackathon demo that runs on the PayPal sandbox. No real money moves and no real bank account is read on this server.

Donations. When you donate with a PayPal sandbox account we store the name and email PayPal returns, the amount and the PayPal ids, to show them in the public ledger and on your donation page.

Proof photos. Photos and receipts a volunteer uploads are stored on the server, shown publicly next to the campaign, and sent to Google's Gemini API for the audit.

Telegram. If you message the bot we keep your chat id to send the updates you asked for.

How long. Everything lives in a temporary database that is wiped whenever the demo server restarts. We use no cookies and no analytics.

Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
