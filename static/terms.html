A demo, not a charity

This site shows how Proof of Good would work. It runs on the PayPal sandbox with test money. Do not enter real card details: the PayPal window here only accepts sandbox accounts and test cards.

The campaigns are examples written for the demo. Their volunteers, cards and bank feeds are simulated and are labelled as such.

The AI auditor can be wrong. In a real deployment its hard rules, such as freezing a card after a cash withdrawal, would run first, and unclear cases would go to a person.

The code is open under the MIT license on GitHub.
